# Step by step logic in math
So, there are many derivatives to calculate and most of them follow either quotient, chain or power rule. Except the X^x (x to the power of x)
To calculate it use and USE ONLY the logartihmic differentiation

Basically
f(x) = X^x 

1) take log of both sides
-> ln(f(x)) = ln(X^x)

2) Then it because its in power it then goes down as multiplication
-> ln(f(x)) = xln(x)

3) Then you just differentiate
-> Use quotient rule for ln(f(x)) and Product rule for xln(x)
This becomes
(f'(x)/f(x)) = ln(x) + 1

then since we want to find out derivative (aka f'(x)) = f(x) * (ln(x) + 1)
BUT DONT FORGET THAT WE STATED THAT f(x) = X^x 

so it becomes 
f'(x) = X^x * (ln(x) + 1)
