## then why tf shit breaks???
Things break when the forces acting on an object exceed its internal strength, not because Newton's third law is violated. The law states that when two objects interact, they exert equal and opposite forces on each other, but these forces act on different objects, so they don't cancel each other out and prevent motion or breaking. Breaking is a matter of material failure: an object fractures when the stress from an applied force surpasses the material's bonding strength.
